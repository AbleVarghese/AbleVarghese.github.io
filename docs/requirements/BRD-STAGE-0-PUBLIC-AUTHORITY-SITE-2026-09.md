# BRD — Stage 0 public authority site · 2026-09

- **Status:** proposed business requirements. No public claim, source edit, or deployment is authorized.
- **Business objective:** let a relevant B2B visitor understand the proposed operations-diagnostic offer, assess fit from public-safe proof, and choose a minimal next action without creating an account, broad CRM, or unbounded data collection.
- **Inputs:** [B2B strategy](https://github.com/AbleVarghese/Consult-Able/blob/Portfolio-consult/docs/strategy/B2B-CONSULTING-GROWTH-EXECUTION-PLAN-2026-09.md) · [approval pack](https://github.com/AbleVarghese/Consult-Able/blob/Portfolio-consult/docs/strategy/PUBLIC-CLAIM-APPROVAL-PACK-2026-09.md) · [open decisions](https://github.com/AbleVarghese/Consult-Able/blob/Portfolio-consult/docs/OPEN-DECISIONS-REGISTER.md).

## Stakeholders and outcomes

| Stakeholder | Need | Success evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevant B2B visitor | Understand relevance, scope, trust, and next step quickly | Can identify the offer, suitable/unsuitable cases, and contact path without an account | No personalized/advisory/financial-service promise |
| Owner | Publish accurate authority material and learn whether the offer attracts fit | Approved wording/projects remain current; qualified-conversation evidence is reviewed | No unsupported employer/customer/outcome claim |
| Estate operator | Host a small reversible static artifact safely | Measured host/backup/recovery/rollback evidence | No inferred Mini readiness |
| Reviewer | Audit why a claim or release is present | Trace from approval to artifact to release record | No private LinkedIn/resumé material in public artifact |

## Business requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Acceptance outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| BR-01 | Present one owner-approved operations-diagnostic proposition | Must | A visitor can reach approved offer scope from the home path |
| BR-02 | Present only public-safe, current, approved proof | Must | Each published proof item has source/disclosure/status/review-date evidence |
| BR-03 | Let a suitable visitor take one minimal, privacy-described next step | Must | The next step has purpose, owner, and data boundary; no account required |
| BR-04 | Keep the release static, accessible, fast, and reversible | Must | Tested artifact/host/rollback evidence exists |
| BR-05 | Produce learning without surveillance or vanity metrics | Should | Owner reviews qualified-conversation/content-feedback signals against the strategy |
| BR-06 | Preserve a clean path to protected operations later | Should | Stage 0 introduces no data/account/runtime shortcut that defeats ADR-002–004 |

## Scope and exclusions

| In scope | Out of scope / trigger |
|---|---|
| Approved home, diagnostic, proof, insight, and contact/privacy content | Account/auth, database, private portal, CRM, uploads, calendar, payment, analytics, AI assistant — each requires a separate measured trigger |
| Static artifact behind the proposed Caddy edge once gates pass | Managed-service substitution, DNS/certificate change, or Mini mutation without approved release gate |
| Evidence-bound content review and public link freshness | Employment/customer/education/financial claims without dedicated approval |

## Business acceptance gate

The BRD advances only when the owner approves relevant wording/proof rows, a source-repository path is measured, the first visitor route maps to PRD/SRD requirements, and the Stage 0 operational gates are passed. Otherwise it remains a business hypothesis, not a delivery commitment.
