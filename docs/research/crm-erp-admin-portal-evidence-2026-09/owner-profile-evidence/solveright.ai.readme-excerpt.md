# SolveRight — decision intelligence, quantified

**[solveright.ai](https://solveright.ai)** · Live platform · Source private (commercial product)

Turns "I think Option A is best" into "Option A scores 82/100 across 12 frameworks with high
confidence, here's the analysis, sensitivity breakdown, and PDF report."

**155 proven decision frameworks**: AHP, TOPSIS, SWOT, Porter's, FMEA, NPV, RICE, Kano and 147
more across 10 categories, run simultaneously against your decision, with deterministic scoring,
cross-framework contradiction detection, and real-time sensitivity analysis.

## The companion: solvemax

**[solvemax.solveright.ai](https://solvemax.solveright.ai)**: a verdict with receipts. Describes a
hard decision → the engine interrogates it, widens the option field, scores with locked weights,
runs **5,000 seeded Monte-Carlo futures**, red-teams its own answer, takes a blind second-pass
audit, and issues a shareable Verdict Slip with a tripwire reminder on the review date.

## Under the hood

Three-phase AI + deterministic hybrid scoring (same input → same score, every time) ·
<100ms sensitivity recompute · multi-model LLM failover chain · Next.js · Stripe · 7 export formats
