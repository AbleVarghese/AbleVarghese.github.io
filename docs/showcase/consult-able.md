# Consult-Able

B2B operations consulting: making consequential workflows more reliable, visible, and easier to govern.

## The offer

I help B2B teams make consequential workflows more reliable, visible, and easier to govern.

Start with a fixed-scope operations diagnostic:

- Map one workflow
- Establish a baseline
- Identify controls
- Decide the next step

## Who it is for

Teams with a business-critical workflow that needs to be more reliable, more visible, or easier to audit — where adding complexity or risk is not an option.

## Approach

Evidence-based, staged, and reversible. Every recommendation is grounded in what is measured, not assumed. The engagement starts small and fixed-scope so both sides can decide the next step from real evidence.

## Status

Consulting practice. Detailed strategy, decision records, and delivery planning are maintained privately.
